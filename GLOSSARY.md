# Zain Khan — Universe

An immersive portfolio: a particle hero, a draggable 3D map of every project, a scroll-drawn timeline and the real commit rhythm.

## Language

**Universe**:
The draggable 3D map of all projects.

**Cluster**:
One of five groups of related projects (AI, Community, Knowledge, Web, Automation).

**Hub**:
The bright node a cluster's projects orbit.

**Rhythm**:
Commits per month, computed from real git history.
